# Out of Scope: Record Revisions

## Context
Per-record version history browsing and restoration.

## Decision
`rejected`

## Reason
Revision APIs are wildly heterogeneous across Backends and demand cooperation the sovereignty pillar forbids assuming. Read-only history would still require normalized prior-version access.

## Consequences
Revisit trigger shared with OD-01; must enter as a declared Capability if ever adopted.
